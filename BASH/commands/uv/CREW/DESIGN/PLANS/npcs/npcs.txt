
PLAN: NPC Management

Objective:
- Define and manage non-player characters (NPCs) for scenarios.

Steps:
1. Define NPC roles and attributes (see SKILLS/assignment_validation.txt for validation).
2. Assign NPCs to scenarios and confirm assignments.
3. Update NPC status after each event and log in NOTEBOOKS/notebooks.txt.

Inputs:
- Scenario requirements
- NPC templates (crew_template_v2.md or similar)

Outputs:
- Updated NPC roster
- Scenario logs

Responsible Agents:
- NPC Manager Agent
- Designer Agent

Notes:
- Regularly review and update NPC attributes as scenarios evolve.
- Follow RULES/always.txt, RULES/never.txt, RULES/sometimes.txt.

Automation & Documentation:
- Automate validation and assignment using referenced SKILLS and templates.
- Document all steps and outcomes in NOTEBOOKS/notebooks.txt.
