Traveller RPG Session Prep Checklist (Adapted from Readiness Review)

1. Review player and referee familiarity with Traveller rules and edition in use.
2. Confirm all character sheets, ship logs, and campaign notes are up to date.
3. Check that all required materials are available:
   - Rulebooks (physical or digital)
   - Dice and tools (e.g., dice roller, character generator)
   - Maps, handouts, and ship plans
4. Verify health and readiness of all participants (players and referee).
5. Conduct a pre-session briefing:
   - Recap previous session
   - Outline session goals and any house rules
6. Complete the session prep checklist:
   - Equipment and materials check
   - Player attendance
   - Scenario and encounter readiness
7. Document session readiness and note any issues or action items.

If readiness is not confirmed, assign corrective actions and schedule a follow-up review.

---

This checklist is based on the CREW/DESIGN/SKILLS/readiness_review.txt process and tailored for Traveller RPG sessions. Use and adapt as needed for your group.
