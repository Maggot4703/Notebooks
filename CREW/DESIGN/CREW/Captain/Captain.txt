Captain

 description of Traveller Captains duties and responsibilities

In the Traveller tabletop RPG universe, the Captain (or Master) of a starship holds a position that is equal parts CEO, diplomat, and tactical commander. Whether you're running a subsidized merchant or a mercenary cruiser, the Captain's primary responsibility is the safety of the vessel and the lives of everyone aboard.

Here is a breakdown of the core duties and responsibilities for a Traveller Captain.
1. Legal and Regulatory Compliance

The Captain is the legal representative of the ship. In the Third Imperium, this means navigating a complex web of interstellar laws.

    Lien and Registration: Ensuring the ship's transponder is active and registration papers are current.

    Customs & Cargo: Taking legal responsibility for all manifested cargo. If the crew is smuggling "unauthorized" goods, the Captain is the one who faces the Imperial sensory net.

    The High Guard: Maintaining order. Under Imperial Law, a Captain has broad "High Justice" authority while in jump space, acting as the judge and jury for any crimes committed on board.

2. Strategic Operations and Navigation

While the Pilot handles the stick and the Navigator plots the course, the Captain makes the "Go/No-Go" decisions.

    Jump Planning: Deciding when to initiate a Jump and ensuring the ship is at least 100 diameters away from any significant gravity well to avoid a misjump.

    Fuel Management: Deciding between buying refined fuel at a starport or "skimming" unrefined fuel from a gas giant (which saves money but risks damaging the drives).

    Mission Selection: In a sandbox game, the Captain chooses which speculative trade goods to buy and which contracts to sign.

3. Financial Oversight

A ship is a massive financial liability. A Captain's most stressful duty is often just keeping the lights on.
Expense Category	Captain's Responsibility
Mortgage	Ensuring the monthly payment (usually 1/240th of the ship's cost) is met.
Maintenance	Budgeting for the annual overhaul (1% of the ship's value).
Life Support	Covering the Cr1,000–Cr2,000 per person per month costs.
Payroll	Managing the "shares" or fixed salaries of the crew.
4. Personnel and Command

The Captain is the glue that keeps a diverse group of Travellers from killing each other during a week-long stint in Jump space.

    Morale: Managing the psychological health of the crew during the isolation of a 168-hour jump.

    Discipline: Resolving conflicts between departments (e.g., the Engineer complaining about the Pilot redlining the maneuvers).

    Crisis Leadership: In combat or during a drive failure, the Captain issues the "Direct" actions, providing bonuses to crew skill checks through the Leadership and Tactics skills.

5. Diplomacy and External Relations

The Captain is the "Face" of the ship when dealing with Starport Authorities, planetary governors, and rival captains.

    Bribery and Negotiation: Knowing when to use a silver tongue and when to use a credit chip to bypass red tape.

    Ship-to-Ship Comms: Handling hails and distress signals in deep space.

The "Captain's Burden"

In Traveller, the Captain is often the character with the highest Social Standing (SOC) or Education (EDU), but the most successful ones are those who master the Leadership, Admin, and Broker skills.

    Pro Tip: A Captain who ignores their Engineer's warnings about a "slightly misaligned" jump drive usually ends up as a permanent resident of a cold, dark corner of the void.
