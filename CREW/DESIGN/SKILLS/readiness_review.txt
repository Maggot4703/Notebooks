SKILL: Readiness Review

Purpose:
- Confirm that all assigned agents are fully prepared for mission execution.

Process:
1. Review training and certification records for all assigned agents.
2. Check equipment and health status.
3. Conduct a readiness review meeting with all stakeholders.
4. Complete the pre-mission checklist.
5. Document readiness status and any issues.

Inputs:
- Training log
- Pre-mission checklist

Outputs:
- Readiness review report

Error Handling:
- If readiness is not confirmed, assign corrective actions and schedule a follow-up review.
